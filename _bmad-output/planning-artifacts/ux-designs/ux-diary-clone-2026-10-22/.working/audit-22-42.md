## Component anatomy and state notes for later screens

- **Entry card (selected Journey)**: white rounded container; top line includes small glyph, letter-spaced type, bold title, right-aligned time; body starts with light gray captured prompt then black answer. Additional chips are outlined white capsules with small icons. Long text wraps over several lines. The source has card variants for compact life events and exercise summaries (42:02,05), and media variants for photos/audio (42:04,06); these variants are reference-only unless an in-scope reflection capture supports the corresponding saved field.
- **Insights banner (selected Journey)**: black rounded rectangle with large white `Insights` or month title, subdued `See All` upper right, monochrome bird/magnifier or a three-column mini-summary. State changes with data and period (42:02–03). Source numeric values `14`, `Okay`, `Some` are **sample data**, not defaults.
- **Day group (selected Journey)**: bold relative day (`Today`, `Yesterday`) and muted date, chevron at far right; selecting opens Daily entries per chosen scope. Cards below are contiguous and tightly spaced. The top `Filter`/`Days` capsule controls are source visuals, but only actions leading to included flows may remain.
- **Navigation shell**: source bottom bar has five slots and central plus. Scoped app can keep the bottom safe-area treatment, black active icon and subdued inactive icon, but needs its own two-target arrangement; selected screenshots still anchor colors and density. The source tab arrangement cannot be transplanted verbatim.
- **Modal**: source shows distinct dimmed sheets, curved creation drawer, and full-screen task pages. Do not label all of them a single `modal-sheet` component. Preserve overlay height and close behavior of a selected screen's own capture.
- **Choices**: segmented category tabs (23:03, 41:02–07) have active underline; standalone radio lists (34:06–07) show black center dot; stars (23:03–04) fill black; emotional tiles (25:03–04) invert black. All require semantic selected state as well as image match when reused.
- **Feedback**: 26:01 and completion flows show three vertical white response cards with hollow, half-filled and full circles. This is a recurring pattern but remains outside this product scope.

## Source-only features and boundaries

Do **not** add Inspirations paths/categories/prompt tiles (22), Favorites (23), independent journaling or prompt picker (24), standalone mood check-in with its AI-adjacent follow-up/breathing upsell (25), feedback collection (26), Library/Collections/Weekly Themes (27–31), breathing/meditation/timers/preferences/sounds/koans/audio walks (32–40), or Search (41). Journey's source also contains unrelated Life Event, Journal, Mood Check-In, Breathing, Meditation and media/audio cards (42:02–06); these demonstrate card grammar only. This follows the brief's explicit implementation table and the user's exclusion of independent journal creation. Keep the brief's onboarding `AI Mentors` **display** exception elsewhere; none of these reference-only screenshots override it.

## Reconciliation with the current DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md

1. The current spine sentence that rules were derived from the **full Stoic corpus** is not proven by these 21 flows alone. Across this audit set, evidence is now explicit. The final spines should cite a full-corpus evidence index before calling them exhaustive.
2. `DESIGN.md` token numbers (`#F3F3F5`, 8/16/24 radii, 4/8/12/16/24/32 spacing) are labeled or presented as if universal, while these screens have visibly distinct background tones, card sizes and compositions. Keep such numbers provisional until measured, and add component-/screen-specific exceptions; 27:04–05, 29:02, 31:03 and 42:03–06 are clear counterexamples to one generic content card.
3. `EXPERIENCE.md` currently describes `modal-sheet` as if it uniformly opens above Today. The visual system contains multiple overlay types (22:02, 23:02, 24:02, 33:02, 34:01–04, 38:01–03), and Profile may be its own selected case. State the selected capture first and identify sheet variants for future use.
4. `EXPERIENCE.md` Journey empty-state `No entries yet` is explicitly an assumption. 42:02 instead displays an action card for creating the first independent journal plus a Life Event. Those are excluded, so a scoped empty state must be designed from system rules and labeled as new; it cannot be called a faithful screenshot transcription.
5. `DESIGN.md` `entry-card` radius 8px and generic card grammar should be verified against 42:03–06 and the other selected detail/list captures. This audit supports rounded cards but does not justify a specific numeric radius.
6. `EXPERIENCE.md` says entry cards derive `tags and media` from saved fields. The user selected only morning/evening reflection authoring; 42:04/06 media/audio may represent unrelated source functionality. Require a field to be captured in an included reflection before presenting its card variant. Never manufacture thumbnails, audio markers, or extra mood records.
7. The scoped two-target bottom nav is a product adaptation, not a direct pixel match for 42:02–06. Its documented visual rule should say what is preserved from the source (safe-area placement, active/inactive icon weight, label scale) and call out the new arrangement as derived, not captured.
