## Component anatomy and state notes

- **Chart card:** header title/description/optional ellipsis; body can hold ring/list, curve, vertical bars, day dots, word cloud, permission state or an explicit no-data state; optional bottom divider and `View All Days`; section label outside. See 43/05–12 and 49/04–06. A chart's empty state is not a generic spinner.
- **Mood dot:** empty outlined circle means no mark, filled blue or rose denotes data, blue circle with check appears in exercise completion, so meaning depends on surrounding legend. Do not re-use a blue check as a mood mark automatically. See 44/01–02, 43/11–12.
- **Period controls:** header selector popover (49/02) and lower fixed pager (49/01,03) are coupled; weekly and monthly plots, titles, date labels, and calendar grid change together.
- **Grouped settings row:** section label → rounded group → 48-ish px rows with separators; right slot can be chevron, value, black switch or muted disabled switch. See 59/02–05, 63/02–04.
- **Nested sheet:** upper left back with parent name and upper right optional Close (59/02; 62/02); preserve scroll and focus on return. Large root titles may compact on scroll (59/02→03).
- **Record detail:** prompt/answer pair is a reading block; the header keeps Back and `More`; long content scrolls; retained media belongs to actual user record (50/02–03). The screens do not show edit mode or its menu; do not present an invented menu as a copied capture.
- **Data-derived state:** 43/02–03 explicitly shows no-data and insufficient-data states. 43/04 and 49/03 are sample historical data, not values to hardcode. Current clone must compute from locally saved reflections and show honest no-data where not enough values exist.
